Why Expand Into Argentina?

Argentina offers exceptional conditions for international companies looking to grow in Latin America.

World-Class Talent Pool

Argentina boasts one of Latin America's highest literacy rates and a deep pool of highly educated professionals in technology, engineering, finance, and creative industries.

Strategic Regional Gateway

Buenos Aires is the commercial and financial capital of the Southern Cone. Operating here gives you access to Mercosur markets — 290 million consumers across Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay, and Chile.

Competitive Operating Costs

Argentina's economic environment offers significant cost advantages for international companies — particularly in professional services, technology development, and back-office operations.

Thriving Tech Ecosystem

Buenos Aires is home to over 11,000 tech companies and a rapidly growing startup scene. Argentina exports over $3B in software and technology services annually to the global market.

Established Legal Framework

Argentina has a sophisticated legal and corporate system based on civil law, with well-established IP protections, commercial courts, and international arbitration frameworks compatible with global standards.

High Growth Potential

With a population of 46 million, a large middle class, and rapid digital adoption, Argentina offers significant growth opportunities across retail, fintech, healthcare, logistics, and professional services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about establishing and operating a company in Argentina.

Argentina offers several entity types for foreign investors. The most common are the SAS (Simplified Joint-Stock Company), ideal for startups and SMEs with a faster registration process; the SRL (LLC), suited for small to mid-size operations; and the SA (Corporation), preferred for larger ventures or those seeking future capital raises. We also handle branch registrations for companies that prefer to extend their existing foreign entity into Argentina. We'll recommend the best structure based on your goals, industry, and ownership setup.

Most company formations are completed within 30 to 60 business days from the moment all required documentation is submitted. The timeline depends on the entity type chosen and government processing times at the IGJ (General Inspection of Justice). SAS entities tend to be faster. SetUP keeps you informed at every milestone so there are no surprises.

No. The entire process can be completed remotely from anywhere in the world. We handle all filings, notary coordination, and government submissions on your behalf. You'll need to provide notarized and apostilled documents from your home country — which we guide you through step by step. No travel required.

The CUIT (Código Único de Identificación Tributaria) is Argentina's Tax Identification Number — equivalent to a Federal Tax ID or EIN. Every company operating legally in Argentina must have a CUIT. It's required to open bank accounts, issue invoices, hire employees, and fulfill all tax obligations with ARCA (formerly AFIP). Obtaining the CUIT is the key milestone in the company formation process — and the moment our professional fee becomes due.

Once registered, your Argentine company will have monthly and annual obligations including: tax filings with ARCA, bookkeeping and financial statements, corporate record-keeping, and annual IGJ filings. Depending on your activity, additional regulatory filings may apply. SetUP's accounting and compliance service covers all of this so you stay fully up to date without the administrative burden.

Yes — through our Employer of Record (EOR) service. We act as the legal employer for your Argentine-based staff while you maintain operational control. This allows you to compliantly hire local talent before completing your full company formation. Once your entity is established, employees can be transferred to your own payroll structure.

Government fees, notary costs, and official disbursements are separate from our professional fees and are billed at exact cost as they arise — with full transparency and receipts provided for every expense. These include IGJ registration fees, notary charges, and ARCA enrollment costs. We provide a full cost estimate at the outset so you can plan accordingly.

Argentine companies are subject to several taxes including Corporate Income Tax (35% on net profit), VAT (21% standard rate), Gross Revenue Tax (provincial — varies by activity and province), and Minimum Presumed Income Tax where applicable. The specific tax burden depends on your industry, revenue structure, and corporate organization. Our tax advisory team can model your projected obligations during the initial consultation.

All official documents are in Spanish as required by Argentine law, and we provide full explanations in English throughout the entire process. We also coordinate closely with Portuguese-speaking clients across the region.
