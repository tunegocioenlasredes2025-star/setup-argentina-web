Market Opportunity

Why Expand Into Argentina?

Argentina is Latin America's third-largest economy — and a single local partner removes all the friction of operating from abroad.

Full Foreign Ownership

Argentina's legal framework allows full ownership by foreign individuals and companies — with the same rights as local residents and predictable corporate structures.

Competitive Start-Up Costs

Argentina offers competitive incorporation timelines and start-up costs compared with the rest of the region — making it one of the most accessible entry points in Latin America.

World-Class Talent Pool

One of Latin America's highest literacy rates and a deep pool of highly educated professionals in technology, law, finance, and creative industries — at competitive salaries.

Established Legal Framework

A sophisticated legal and corporate system based on civil law, with predictable entity structures (SRL, SAS, SA) and well-established compliance procedures through IGJ and ARCA.

Thriving Tech & Services Ecosystem

Buenos Aires is home to a booming tech and professional services sector, with Argentina exporting over $3B annually in software and digital services to global markets.

One Partner, Zero Friction

Operating from abroad is complex. A single local partner for legal and accounting removes the friction — whether you're formalizing remotely or scaling your presence on the ground.

The most common entity types are the SAS (Simplified Joint-Stock Company, ideal for startups and SMEs), the SRL (LLC, suited for small to mid-size operations), and the SA (Corporation, for larger ventures). We also handle branch registrations. We'll recommend the best structure based on your goals, industry, and ownership setup.

The CUIT is Argentina's Tax Identification Number — required to open bank accounts, issue invoices, hire employees, and fulfill all tax obligations with ARCA. Obtaining the CUIT is the key milestone in the formation process and unlocks your company's full operational capacity.

Once registered, your company will have monthly and annual obligations including ARCA tax filings, bookkeeping, corporate record-keeping, and annual IGJ renewals. SetUP's accounting and compliance service covers all of this so you stay fully up to date without the administrative burden.
