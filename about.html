What Sets Us Apart

We built SetUP Argentina because international clients deserve better than generalist firms with no skin in the game.

01

You Pay Only on Success

Our professional fees are deferred until your Argentine Tax ID (CUIT) is successfully issued. You take zero financial risk on our work — this is how we prove our commitment to your success.

02

Senior Professionals Only

Your case is handled directly by a corporate attorney or tax advisor — not a junior associate or assistant. Every client gets senior-level attention from day one.

03

No Call Centers. No Bots.

You communicate directly with the person handling your case — via email, WhatsApp, or video call. Response within 24 business hours, always.

04

Bilingual from Day One

All advisory and communication are provided in English. No translation delays, no miscommunication between you and your advisor.

05

Deep Local Expertise

We operate under the jurisdiction of IGJ and ARCA — Argentina's primary regulatory bodies. Our team has direct, hands-on experience navigating Buenos Aires' legal and tax environment.

06

Global Client Perspective

We've advised clients from the US, Brazil, Europe, and Asia. We understand your home jurisdiction and bridge the gap between your regulatory background and Argentine law.
