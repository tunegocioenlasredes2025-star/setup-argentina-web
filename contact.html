Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

The most common entity types are the SAS (Simplified Joint-Stock Company, ideal for startups and SMEs), the SRL (LLC, suited for small to mid-size operations), and the SA (Corporation, for larger ventures). We also handle branch registrations. We'll recommend the best structure based on your specific situation.

Most formations are completed within 30 to 60 business days from document submission. The timeline depends on entity type and IGJ processing times. SetUP keeps you informed at every milestone.

Our professional advisory fees are only due once your Argentine Tax ID (CUIT) has been successfully issued. You carry zero financial risk on our professional services during the registration process. Government fees, notary costs, and disbursements are separate and billed at exact cost with full receipts.

Once registered, your company has monthly and annual obligations including ARCA tax filings, bookkeeping, corporate record-keeping, and annual IGJ renewals. SetUP's accounting and compliance service covers all of this so you stay fully compliant without the administrative burden.

Yes — through our Employer of Record (EOR) service. We act as the legal employer for your Argentine staff while you maintain operational control. Employees can transfer to your own entity once registration is complete.

Argentine companies are subject to Corporate Income Tax (35% on net profit), VAT (21%), Gross Revenue Tax (provincial, varies by activity), and others. The specific tax burden depends on your industry and corporate structure. We'll model your projected obligations during the initial consultation.
