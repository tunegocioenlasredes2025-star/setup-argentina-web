IGJ Registration

Company Formation

We handle the complete registration of your Argentine legal entity — LLC (SRL), SAS (Simplified Joint-Stock Company), SA (Corporation), or Branch of a foreign company — under the General Inspectorate of Justice (IGJ). Every filing, notary coordination, and government submission is managed by our team. You don't need to travel to Argentina.

  • SRL, SAS, SA, and branch registration
  • Full IGJ documentation and filing
  • Notary coordination and apostille guidance
  • Corporate bylaws drafting
  • Registered fiscal address included
ARCA / CUIT

Tax ID Registration (CUIT)

The CUIT (Código Único de Identificación Tributaria) is Argentina's Tax ID — required for all business operations, bank accounts, and invoicing. We handle the complete ARCA enrollment process for your entity. Our professional fee is only due once your CUIT has been successfully issued. Zero financial risk before this milestone.

  • Complete ARCA enrollment
  • CUIT issuance for the Argentine entity
  • Tax category registration (VAT, Income Tax)
  • No fee until successful issuance
Statutory Compliance

Legal Representation

Argentine law requires foreign entities to designate a qualified local representative. Our team fulfills this statutory requirement — acting as your legal representative before the IGJ, ARCA, and other regulatory bodies. Every correspondence, notification, and filing is managed professionally on your behalf.

  • Qualified local representative appointment
  • Regulatory correspondence management
  • IGJ and ARCA liaison
  • Corporate secretary services
Monthly Filings

Accounting & Tax Compliance

Once your company is operating, Argentine law requires monthly and annual filings with ARCA (formerly AFIP). We manage your complete accounting — bookkeeping, VAT returns, income tax filings, balance sheets, and all regulatory reporting — so you stay compliant without the administrative burden.

  • Monthly ARCA tax filings (VAT, Income Tax, Gross Revenue)
  • Bookkeeping and financial statements
  • Annual balance sheet preparation
  • Corporate record-keeping (IGJ annual filings)
  • Audit preparation support
Labor Compliance

Payroll Services

Argentine labor law is complex, with strict requirements for employment contracts, social security contributions, and salary payments. We manage your complete payroll — from contract drafting to monthly salary processing and all mandatory employer contributions.

  • Employment contract drafting and registration
  • Monthly salary processing and payslips
  • Social security (SIPA/ANSES) contributions
  • Vacation, aguinaldo, and severance calculations
  • Full Argentine labor law compliance
EOR Services

Employer of Record (EOR)

Need to hire Argentine talent before your entity is fully established? Our Employer of Record service lets you onboard local employees compliantly from day one — we act as the legal employer while you maintain full operational control of the work. Once your entity is ready, employees can transfer seamlessly.

  • Compliant employment without a local entity
  • Full HR administration and payroll
  • Labor law compliance guaranteed
  • Seamless entity transfer when ready
Work Permits

Immigration & Visas

Moving executives or key personnel to Argentina requires navigating immigration law carefully. We advise on the right visa category, prepare all required documentation, and guide foreign nationals through the residency permit process — making relocation smooth and compliant.

  • Work visa applications and renewals
  • Temporary and permanent residency permits
  • Executive relocation advisory
  • MERCOSUR and treaty-based residence options
IGJ · ARCA

Corporate Compliance

Running a company in Argentina involves ongoing obligations with multiple regulatory bodies. We manage all recurring compliance requirements — from annual IGJ filings and board meeting minutes to ARCA reporting and corporate record-keeping — so your company stays in good legal standing year after year.

  • Annual IGJ filings and corporate renewals
  • Board and shareholder meeting minutes
  • Statutory books maintenance
  • Regulatory change monitoring and alerts
Cross-Border

Legal & Tax Advisory

Beyond formation and compliance, international businesses often need strategic legal and tax guidance — contract drafting, cross-border structuring, corporate governance, and transaction support. Our attorneys provide independent advice tailored to your home jurisdiction and Argentine law.

  • Commercial contract drafting and review
  • Cross-border tax structuring
  • Corporate governance advisory
  • International transaction support
  • IP and data protection basics
Buenos Aires Address

Fiscal Domicile

Every Argentine company must have a registered fiscal address. We provide a registered domicile in Buenos Aires (CABA), fulfilling the legal requirement without needing to lease or manage a physical office space — ideal for remote-first or asset-light operations.

  • Registered fiscal address in CABA
  • Mail handling and forwarding
  • Official correspondence management
  • Can be upgraded to physical office if needed
